Credit Policy Guidelines 
Purpose 
This document defines the organization's credit decision framework and approval 
recommendations. 
The final customer classification is based on the overall Credit Risk Assessment Score. 
Low Risk Customer 
Score Above 80 
Risk Classification 
Low Risk 
Recommended Credit Limit 
USD 5,000,000 
Recommended Payment Terms 
60 Days 
Business Interpretation 
Customers in this category demonstrate strong financial health, satisfactory compliance results, 
and lower overall risk exposure. 
Medium Risk Customer 
Score Between 50 and 80 
Risk Classification 
Medium Risk 
Recommended Credit Limit 
USD 2,000,000 
Recommended Payment Terms 
30 Days 
Business Interpretation 
Customers demonstrate acceptable financial performance but may require additional 
monitoring. 
High Risk Customer 
Score Below 50 
Risk Classification 
High Risk 
Recommended Credit Limit 
No Credit Facility 
Recommended Payment Terms 
Advance Payment Required 
Business Interpretation 
Customers demonstrate elevated business, financial, or compliance risk. 
Policy Exceptions 
Management approval is required for exceptions to standard credit policies. 
Approved credit limits may be adjusted by authorized personnel. 
Business approval does not override compliance requirements. 
Inactive companies should not receive credit approval. 
Applications with missing mandatory documentation should be placed on hold until 
documentation is received. 
Any sanctions or watchlist match requires manual review.
