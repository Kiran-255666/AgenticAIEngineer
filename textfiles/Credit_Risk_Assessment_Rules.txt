CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT RULES

Purpose
-------
These rules are used to assess the credit risk of a client company and determine the appropriate credit limit and payment terms.

1. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION
------------------------

Verify that both of the following documents are available:

- Company Registration Certificate
- GST Certificate

Both documents must be present to pass the document verification step.

Compare the client company name with the company name shown on both certificates.

The company names must match exactly.

If either required document is missing, or the company name does not match, the compliance check fails.

2. COMPANY LEGAL STATUS
-----------------------

Check the company's status through the MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs).

Accepted status:

- Active

An Inactive status should be treated as a compliance concern and considered during the final risk assessment.

3. SANCTIONS AND COMPLIANCE SCREENING
-------------------------------------

Check the company against the Dow Jones sanctions database and relevant compliance records.

Look for:

- Sanctions
- Watchlists
- Adverse compliance records

A confirmed sanctions or compliance match is a rejection condition.

4. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
-----------------------

Confirm that audited or summarized financial statements are available for the last two financial years.

Use the latest financial year for the primary financial ratio calculations.

Calculate:

Current Ratio
--------------
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

Debt-to-Equity Ratio
--------------------
Debt-to-Equity = Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity

Net Profit Margin
-----------------
Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100

5. EXTERNAL CREDIT BUREAU
-------------------------

Obtain the latest Dun & Bradstreet (D&B) credit score or rating available for the company.

Use the latest available score when calculating the credit-risk score.

6. INDUSTRY RISK
----------------

Assign an industry risk rating using the following Industry Risk Matrix.

LOW RISK:
- Utilities
- Government
- Healthcare

Industry score: 15

MEDIUM RISK:
- Manufacturing
- FMCG distribution
- IT services

Industry score: 8

HIGH RISK:
- Construction
- Real estate developers
- Airlines
- Commodity trading
- Startups
- Mining & Metal

Industry score: 3

7. CREDIT RISK SCORECARD
------------------------

The total score is 100 points.

Compliance: 20 points
Liquidity (Current Ratio): 20 points
Leverage (Debt-to-Equity): 15 points
Profitability (Net Profit Margin): 15 points
External Credit Bureau: 15 points
Industry Risk: 15 points

COMPLIANCE
----------

No sanctions/compliance match:
20 points

Confirmed sanctions/compliance match:
0 points

A confirmed match is a rejection condition.

CURRENT RATIO
-------------

Current Ratio >= 1.5:
20 points

Current Ratio 1.2–1.49:
15 points

Current Ratio 1.0–1.19:
8 points

Current Ratio < 1.0:
0 points

DEBT-TO-EQUITY
--------------

Debt-to-Equity <= 1.5:
15 points

Debt-to-Equity 1.51–2.0:
10 points

Debt-to-Equity 2.01–3.0:
5 points

Debt-to-Equity > 3.0:
0 points

NET PROFIT MARGIN
-----------------

Net Profit Margin >= 8%:
15 points

Net Profit Margin 4–7.99%:
10 points

Net Profit Margin 1–3.99%:
5 points

Net Profit Margin < 1% or a loss:
0 points

D&B CREDIT SCORE
----------------

D&B score >= 700:
15 points

D&B score 650–699:
10 points

D&B score 600–649:
5 points

D&B score < 600:
0 points

INDUSTRY RISK
-------------

Low Risk:
15 points

Medium Risk:
8 points

High Risk:
3 points

8. FINAL RISK CLASSIFICATION
----------------------------

Calculate the total score by adding all six scorecard components.

TOTAL SCORE > 80:
Risk Rating: Low

Recommended credit limit: $5,000,000
Recommended payment terms: 60 days

TOTAL SCORE 50–80:
Risk Rating: Medium

Recommended credit limit: $2,000,000
Recommended payment terms: 30 days

TOTAL SCORE < 50:
Risk Rating: High

Credit recommendation: No credit
Payment requirement: Advance payment

9. FINAL ASSESSMENT
-------------------

The final assessment should include:

- Document verification result
- Company name match result
- MCA legal status
- Sanctions/compliance screening result
- Financial ratios
- D&B credit score
- Industry risk rating
- Score for each scorecard category
- Total score out of 100
- Final risk rating
- Recommended credit limit
- Recommended payment terms

The agent should clearly explain how each score was calculated and should not invent missing company information.

WORKED EXAMPLE
--------------

Example input:

Current Ratio: 1.56
Debt-to-Equity: 1.86
Net Profit Margin: 5.56%
D&B Credit Score: 720
Industry: Utilities
Compliance: No confirmed match

Score calculation:

Compliance = 20
Current Ratio = 20
Debt-to-Equity = 10
Net Profit Margin = 10
D&B Credit Score = 15
Industry Risk = 15

Total = 90/100

Final Risk Rating: Low

Recommended credit limit: $5,000,000
Recommended payment terms: 60 days
