Credit Risk Assessment Scorecard 
Purpose 
The Credit Risk Assessment Scorecard provides a standardized framework for evaluating 
customer credit risk. 
The scorecard combines compliance verification, financial performance, external credit 
information, and industry risk to determine an overall risk profile. 
Maximum Possible Score: 100

Frequently Asked Questions 
What happens if a sanctions match is identified? 
Any sanctions or compliance concern results in a compliance score of 0 and requires manual 
review. 
Can a customer proceed if compliance checks fail? 
No. Compliance concerns must be resolved before a final credit decision can be made.

Definition 
Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities 
Example 
Current Assets = 150,000 
Current Liabilities = 100,000 
Current Ratio = 1.50 
Score Awarded = 20 
Frequently Asked Questions 
What score is assigned when Current Ratio exceeds 1.5? 
20 points. 
What score is assigned for a Current Ratio of 1.3? 
15 points. 
What does a Current Ratio below 1.0 indicate? 
The company may have difficulty meeting short-term obligations.

Assessment Guidelines 
Use the most recent financial statements available. 
Validate all calculations before assigning scores. 
Missing financial data should be escalated for manual review. 
Compliance checks must always be completed first.
