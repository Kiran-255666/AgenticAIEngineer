Industry Risk Matrix 
Purpose 
The Industry Risk Matrix provides a standardized approach for assessing industry-related 
risk during credit evaluations. 
Industry risk represents the likelihood that economic, market, regulatory, or operational 
conditions could negatively affect a company's ability to meet its financial obligations.

Industry Assessment Guidelines 
The primary source of revenue should determine the industry classification. 
If a company operates across multiple industries, classify it according to the business 
segment that generates the highest revenue. 
Industry classification should be reviewed periodically to ensure accuracy. 
If insufficient information exists to determine an industry classification, the application 
should be escalated for manual review. 
Industry risk should not be used as the sole basis for approving or rejecting a customer. 
Industry risk is one component of the overall credit assessment process.
