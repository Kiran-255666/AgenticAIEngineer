Industry Risk Matrix
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2025-09-18

Purpose

This document defines the industry risk categories used during credit risk assessments.

Industry Risk Ratings

Low Risk Industries
- Utilities
- Government
- Healthcare

Medium Risk Industries
- Manufacturing
- FMCG Distribution
- IT Services

High Risk Industries
- Construction
- Real Estate Developers
- Airlines
- Commodity Trading
- Startups
- Mining & Metal

Assessment Guidance

Industry risk is one factor within the overall credit assessment process.

Lower-risk industries typically demonstrate more stable demand, predictable cash flows, and lower business volatility.

Higher-risk industries may experience cyclical demand, market volatility, project-based revenue fluctuations, funding challenges, or regulatory uncertainty.

Review industry classification carefully before assigning a risk rating.
