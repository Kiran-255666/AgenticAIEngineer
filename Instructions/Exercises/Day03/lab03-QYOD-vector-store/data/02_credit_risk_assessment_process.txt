Credit Risk Assessment Process
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2025-10-02

Objective

Assess customer creditworthiness using document verification, compliance validation, financial analysis, external bureau data, and industry risk evaluation.

Process Overview

Step 1
Verify Company Registration Certificate and GST Certificate are available.

Step 2
Confirm the client company name matches both submitted certificates.

Step 3
Verify company legal status through the MCA database.

Step 4
Review sanctions, watchlists, and compliance findings from approved compliance screening sources.

Step 5
Collect financial statements for the previous two financial years.

Step 6
Calculate key financial ratios from the latest financial year:
- Current Ratio
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- Net Profit Margin

Step 7
Retrieve the latest external credit bureau score.

Step 8
Assign an Industry Risk Rating using the Industry Risk Matrix.

Step 9
Determine the final risk outcome and recommended credit terms.

Day 3 Focus

Day 3 retrieval exercises focus only on Step 8 Industry Risk Rating.
Other assessment steps are included for business context only.
