## 3. Document Verification Policy

Every company must have the following documents:

1. Company Registration Certificate.
2. GST Certificate.

The verification process must check:

- Whether both documents are available.
- Whether the documents are readable and valid.
- Whether the company name matches the registered customer name.
- Whether the registration number matches the company record.
- Whether the GSTIN matches the company record.
- Whether the MCA registration status is active.

## 4. Verification Status Definitions

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| Verified | Both required documents are available, readable, and consistent with the company record. |
| Pending | One or more verification checks are incomplete. |
| Failed | A required document is missing, invalid, or contains conflicting information. |
| Manual Review | An exception requires review by a credit analyst. |

## 5. Important Data Handling Rules

- Use the exact company names specified in this document across all assessment records.
- Do not modify registration numbers or GSTINs when referencing these companies.
- Use FY2025 when retrieving the corresponding financial information.
- Use the external credit bureau scores recorded for each company.
- Do not assume that document verification automatically means credit approval.
- Keep industry risk classification separate from document verification status.
- Treat every identifier and verification result in this document as synthetic sample data.
