CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT RULES

Document Verification
---------------------
The following documents are required:
1. Company Registration Certificate
2. GST Certificate

Both documents must be available.

The company name must match across the provided documents.

Company registration status should be reviewed.
An Active status is acceptable.
An Inactive status requires attention.

Compliance
----------
Check for sanctions and compliance records.

A confirmed match in sanctions or compliance records results
in rejection.

Financial Analysis
------------------
Use the latest available financial year for ratio analysis.

Current Ratio:
Current Assets / Current Liabilities

Debt-to-Equity Ratio:
Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity

Net Profit Margin:
(Net Profit / Revenue) × 100

External Credit Bureau
----------------------
Use the latest available external credit bureau score or rating.

Industry Risk
-------------
Industry risk categories:

Low Risk:

- Government
- Healthcare

Medium Risk:
- Manufacturing
- FMCG distribution
- IT services

High Risk:
- Construction
- Real estate developers
- Airlines
- Commodity trading
- Startups
- Mining & Metal

---------------------

Medium Risk:

Payment terms up to 30 days
