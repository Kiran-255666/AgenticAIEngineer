## 4. Financial Ratio Formulas

### 4.1 Current Ratio

Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

This ratio measures the company's ability to meet short-term financial obligations.

### 4.2 Debt-to-Equity Ratio

Debt-to-Equity Ratio = Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity

This ratio measures the company's reliance on borrowed funds compared with shareholders' capital.

### 4.3 Net Profit Margin

Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100

This ratio measures the percentage of revenue retained as net profit.
